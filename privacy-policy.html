Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Habit Care (“we”, “our”, or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Habit Care mobile application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information — such as email address or sign-in details you provide when creating an account.
  • Habit and usage data — habits you create, completion history, preferences, and app settings.
  • Device information — device type, operating system, and basic diagnostics to improve stability.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain Habit Care features
  • Sync your habits and progress across devices (where available)
  • Improve the app and fix issues
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we work to safeguard your information using industry-standard practices.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service providers who help us operate the app (for example hosting or analytics), only as needed to provide the service, or when required by law.

6. Your Choices

You may update account details in the app where available. To delete your account and associated data, see How to delete User Account.

7. Children’s Privacy

Habit Care is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@habitcare.app.
