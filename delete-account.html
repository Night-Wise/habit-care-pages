How to delete User Account

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Overview

You can request deletion of your Habit Care account and associated personal data. Once deleted, your habits, progress, and account details cannot be recovered.

Delete from the app

  1. Open the Habit Care app.
  2. Go to Settings (or your profile).
  3. Select Account or Privacy.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

If you do not see a delete option yet, use the email method below.

Delete by email

Send a request from the email address linked to your account:

  1. Email support@habitcare.app with the subject line: Delete Habit Care Account.
  2. Include the email or username used for your Habit Care account.
  3. We will process your request and confirm when your account has been deleted (typically within 7 days).

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile and sign-in details
  • Habit lists and completion history
  • App preferences tied to your account

Some anonymized or aggregated data may be retained where required for legal, security, or operational reasons. See our Privacy Policy for more detail.
